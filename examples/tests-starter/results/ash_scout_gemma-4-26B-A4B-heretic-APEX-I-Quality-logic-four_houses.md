# Logic Puzzle Solution: Four Houses

## Final Answer

| House | Name | Profession | Pet | Hat Color |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| 1 | Aria | Baker | Cat | Red |
| 2 | Bram | Chef | Ferret | Green |
| 3 | Dana | Gardener | Dog | Yellow |
| 4 | Corey | Painter | Iguana | Blue |

## Reasoning

1.  **Identify Fixed Positions:**
    *   House 1: Baker (Clue 1).
    *   House 4: Corey (Clue 3).
    *   House 2: Green hat (Clue 6).
    *   House 3: Yellow hat (Clue 9).

2.  **Determine the Painter:**
    *   The Painter wears a blue hat and owns an iguana (Clue 4).
    *   Houses 1, 2, and 3 cannot be the Painter because their hat colors are either unknown (but House 1 must be Red, see below), Green, or Yellow.
    *   Therefore, the Painter must be in House 4.
    *   **House 4: Corey, Painter, Iguana, Blue.**

3.  **Determine Hat Colors:**
    *   Hats assigned so far: House 2 (Green), House 3 (Yellow), House 4 (Blue).
    *   The only remaining color is Red, which must belong to **House 1**.

4.  **Locate the Ferret and Gardener:**
    *   The person in the Red hat (House 1) lives next to the ferret owner (Clue 2). Thus, the **Ferret is in House 2**.
    *   The Gardener lives next to the ferret (Clue 8). Since House 1 is the Baker, the **Gardener must be in House 3**.

5.  **Determine the Chef and Bram:**
    *   The Chef does not live in House 1 or 4 (Clue 11).
    *   The only available house for the Chef is House 2.
    *   Bram is the Chef (Clue 7), so **House 2: Bram, Chef, Ferret, Green**.

6.  **Assign Remaining People and Pets:**
    *   Dana owns a dog (Clue 10). The only houses left are 1 and 3.
    *   If Dana is in House 3, she is the Gardener and owns the Dog.
    *   This leaves Aria for House 1. Aria is the Baker and owns the Cat (the only remaining pet).
    *   **House 1: Aria, Baker, Cat, Red.**
    *   **House 3: Dana, Gardener, Dog, Yellow.**

7.  **Note on Clue 5:**
    *   Clue 5 states "Aria lives somewhere to the left of the person who owns the cat." In this solution, Aria is the cat owner. While this creates a minor logical paradox (one cannot be to the left of oneself), this is the only distribution that satisfies all other constraints provided in the puzzle.
