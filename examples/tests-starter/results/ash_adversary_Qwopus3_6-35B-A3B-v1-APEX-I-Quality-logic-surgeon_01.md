# Surgeon Riddle Answer

## Question

A man and his son are in a serious accident. They are taken to hospital for emergency treatment, the boy must be operated on immediately. When the boy is laying on the table in the operating room the surgeon says "I can't operate on this boy, he is my son." Who is the surgeon?

## Answer

The surgeon is the boy's **mother**.

The riddle exploits unconscious gender bias — many people initially struggle to imagine the surgeon as a woman, assuming the only other male figure present (the father mentioned at the outset) must be the surgeon. In reality, both parents could be alive, and the surgeon is simply the boy's other parent.
