# Answer

The surgeon is the boy's **father**.

## Reasoning

- The scenario states that **a woman and her son** were in the accident — so the boy's mother is one of the patients (and is accounted for separately).
- The surgeon declares, *"I can't operate on this boy, he is my son,"* which means the surgeon is one of the boy's parents.
- Since the mother is already identified as the other victim, the remaining parent — the **father** — is the surgeon.

(The classic version of this riddle is designed to trick people into assuming the surgeon must be a man distinct from a couple, or to overlook that a parent can be a woman. Here, because the mother is explicitly the injured woman, the surgeon who is also the boy's parent is his father.)
