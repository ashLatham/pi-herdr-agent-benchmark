# Surgeon Riddle Answer

## Question

A woman and her son are in a serious accident. They are taken to hospital for emergency treatment, the boy must be operated on immediately. When the boy is laying on the table in the operating room the surgeon says 'I can't operate on this boy, he is my son.' Who is the surgeon?

## Answer

The surgeon is the boy's **mother**.

## Explanation

This is a classic lateral thinking puzzle designed to reveal unconscious gender bias. Many people instinctively assume the surgeon is the boy's father, overlooking the possibility that the surgeon is his mother. Both parents can be involved — one was injured in the accident, and the other happens to be the surgeon on duty.
